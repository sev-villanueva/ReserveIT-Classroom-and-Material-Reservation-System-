2. Part A – Backup Planning
2.1 Database & Objects Scoped
Database Name: pawpaw_dbCore Schemas & Tables:public.clients (client profile info & contact details)public.patients (pet records & medical history links)public.veterinarians (staff doctor profiles)public.appointments (booking and scheduling logs)public.medical_records (consultation and prescription logs)public.inventory (clinic stock and medicine supply)public.invoices (billing and transaction history)public.audit_logs (system operation logs)
2.2 Backup Method Rationale
Method: pg_dump with PostgreSQL Custom Archive Format (-Fc).Justification: Custom archive format is compressed by default, permits selective restoration of individual tables/schemas via pg_restore, and provides strict integrity checks during extraction.
2.3 Storage & Access Control
Local Storage Path: C:\postgres_backups\pawpaw_secure\ (excluded from public VCS via .gitignore).Access Control: Restricted via filesystem permissions exclusively to the DBA user and the dedicated pawpaw_dba PostgreSQL account.
2.4 Proposed Backup Schedule & Retention Policy
Daily Full Backups: Executed at 01:00 AM off-peak hours using pg_dump -Fc. Retention: 30 days.Weekly Backups: Archiving on Sundays. Retention: 12 months.
2.5 Recovery Objectives (RPO & RTO)
Recovery Point Objective (RPO): Maximum 15 minutes of acceptable data loss.Recovery Time Objective (RTO): Maximum 1 hour of operational downtime for full restoration into a secondary instance.
2.6 Strategy for Recreating Activity 4 Roles & Privileges
Since PostgreSQL single database dumps (pg_dump) do not capture cluster-wide global roles, roles and permissions are re-applied post-restoration using pre-written Activity 4 DDL initialization scripts.
